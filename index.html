Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Ignition Realm ("we", "us", or "our") develops and publishes Word Search (the "Game").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Word Search. We designed the current version of the Game to operate primarily offline and to keep gameplay information on your device.

2. Information We Collect

The current version of Word Search does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to us.

The Game does not intentionally collect or transmit the following types of personal information to Ignition Realm:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your contacts
  • Your photos or videos
  • Your microphone or audio recordings
  • Your camera data
  • Your precise location
  • Your physical address
  • Your account passwords
  • Payment or financial information

3. Locally Stored Gameplay Data

Word Search stores certain gameplay information locally on your device. This may include gameplay progress and the total number of games played.

This information is used to provide the Game's functionality and is stored on the device rather than being transmitted to Ignition Realm's servers.

Because this information is stored locally, deleting the Game or clearing its application data may remove the stored gameplay information.

4. Advertising

The current version of Word Search does not display advertisements.

The application package includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK for potential advertising functionality. However, advertising is disabled in the current version of Word Search. The Game does not initialize the advertising SDK or request, load, or display advertisements while advertising is disabled.

If advertising functionality is enabled in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate before that version is released.

5. Third-Party Services

The current version of Word Search includes third-party software components that are used to provide application functionality.

In particular, the application package contains the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Advertising functionality is disabled in the current version, as described above.

Third-party services may have their own privacy policies and terms. Where a third-party service is activated in a future version of the Game, the relevant disclosures will be reflected in this Privacy Policy.

6. Data Sharing

Ignition Realm does not sell your personal information.

The current version of Word Search does not intentionally transmit the locally stored gameplay information described in this policy to Ignition Realm or to third parties.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to limit the collection and handling of information by the Game. Since the current version stores gameplay information locally on your device, the security of that information is also affected by the security of your device and its operating system.

8. Children's Privacy

Word Search does not require users to provide personal information to play the Game.

We do not knowingly request personal information from children through the Game. If you believe that personal information has been provided to us through the Game, please contact us using the contact information available on the Google Play Store listing so that we can review the matter.

9. Your Choices

You can stop using Word Search at any time. Because gameplay information is stored locally, you can generally remove locally stored Game data through your Android device's application settings or by uninstalling the Game.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game's functionality or data practices change.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

11. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of Word Search, please contact Ignition Realm using the developer contact information provided on the Word Search Google Play Store listing.

Summary: Word Search V1 does not require an account, does not display advertisements, and keeps its gameplay information locally on the device. Advertising functionality is disabled in this version even though the Google Mobile Ads SDK is included in the application package.
